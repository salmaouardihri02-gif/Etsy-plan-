## FULL DESCRIPTION

Those small bumps on the back of your arms are not dry skin, not allergies, and not something you did wrong. It's keratosis pilaris — and it's genetic, extremely common, and completely manageable.

KP happens when keratin (a protein) builds up and blocks hair follicles, creating rough, bumpy patches. Most people scrub harder and moisturise more — which doesn't help because they're targeting the wrong thing. This guide explains exactly what works and why.
