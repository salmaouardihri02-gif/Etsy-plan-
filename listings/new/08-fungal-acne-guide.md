You've tried every acne product and nothing works. You might not have regular acne at all.

Fungal acne (technically called Malassezia folliculitis) is one of the most commonly misdiagnosed skin conditions. It looks like acne. It appears in the same places as acne. But it's caused by a yeast overgrowth — and most acne products make it worse because they feed the yeast.

This guide teaches you how to identify fungal acne, which products to avoid (the list will surprise you), and the exact routine to clear it.
